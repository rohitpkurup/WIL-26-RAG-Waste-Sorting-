TITLE: Food and garden organics (FOGO) guidance
SOURCE ORGANISATION: Sustainability Victoria (Victorian Government)
SOURCE PAGE: Keep rubbish out of your food and garden organics bin
LAST UPDATED: 28 August 2025

The lime green FOGO bin is intended for accepted food scraps and garden clippings.

Keep contaminants out, including fruit stickers, plastic food packaging, cling wrap, resealable bags, containers, and takeaway packaging and utensils, including some products labelled compostable.

Correctly separated food and garden material can be processed into compost. Check local council guidance because accepted items may vary.
