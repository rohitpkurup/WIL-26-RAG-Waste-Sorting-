TITLE: Detox Your Home - free household chemical drop-off events
SOURCE ORGANISATION: Department of Energy, Environment and Climate Action (DEECA), Victorian Government
SOURCE PAGE: Detox Your Home
SOURCE URL: https://www.environment.vic.gov.au/hazardous-household-chemicals/detox-your-home
LAST UPDATED: 22 July 2026

Detox Your Home is a free Victorian Government service for disposing of hazardous household chemicals safely. Events are staffed by trained chemists. Any Victorian can attend any event, but you must register online first.

Detox Your Home accepts acids and alkalis, aerosols, spray paint, automotive paint, bleach, brake fluid, coolant, small camping-stove butane canisters, fertiliser, fuel, hand sanitiser, herbicides and weed killers, insect sprays, pesticides, rat poison and other poisons, machine oil, nail polish remover, oven cleaner, photographic chemicals, pool chemicals, rust remover, transmission fluid, turpentine, paint thinners and strippers, wood preservatives and finishes, and non-halon fire extinguishers.

Detox Your Home does not accept motor oil, gas bottles or cylinders (other than small camping canisters), household paint, or yellow halon fire extinguishers. Heating oil, mercury thermometers and thermostats, helium canisters and propane cylinders need approval before you bring them.

To transport chemicals to an event, put them in an empty car boot or tray, keep them in their original packaging where possible, and label repackaged or unknown substances. The largest container accepted is 20 kilograms or 20 litres. Stay in your vehicle while staff unload; it takes about 10 to 15 minutes.
