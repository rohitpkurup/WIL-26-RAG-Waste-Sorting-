TITLE: Victoria's 4-bin recycling system
SOURCE ORGANISATION: Sustainability Victoria (Victorian Government)
SOURCE PAGE: Victoria's 4-bin recycling system
LAST UPDATED: 21 August 2025

Victoria's household waste system uses four main streams:
- Lime green: food and garden organics (FOGO).
- Purple: glass bottles and jars.
- Yellow: mixed recycling.
- Red: general rubbish.

Separate glass collection reduces contamination in mixed recycling. Food scraps and garden clippings can be separated from landfill waste and processed into compost. Check local council guidance because rollout and accepted materials can vary.
