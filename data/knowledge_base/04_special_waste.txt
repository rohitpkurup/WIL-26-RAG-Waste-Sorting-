TITLE: Items to keep out of household recycling and bins
SOURCE ORGANISATION: Sustainability Victoria (Victorian Government)
SOURCE PAGE: Check what goes into your bins on your council website

Soft plastics that can be scrunched into a ball should generally be kept out of household recycling.

Clothing, shoes and textiles can interfere with recycling machinery. Polystyrene packaging and boxes should not be placed in household recycling.

Where a FOGO service is available, food scraps can go into the appropriate organics stream.

E-waste items with a cord, plug or battery should not go in household bins.

Household chemicals such as some cleaners, bleach, fertiliser, car-care chemicals and nail-polish products should not be placed in household bins. Use an appropriate collection or drop-off service.

Check council guidance because local services can differ.
