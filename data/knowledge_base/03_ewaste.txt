TITLE: E-waste disposal guidance
SOURCE ORGANISATION: Sustainability Victoria (Victorian Government)
SOURCE PAGE: Recycle e-waste
LAST UPDATED: 27 November 2025

E-waste must not be placed in household rubbish or recycling bins in Victoria. Take it to an appropriate e-waste collection or drop-off point.

E-waste generally includes unwanted items with a plug, battery or power cord. Examples include batteries, computers, laptops, mobile phones, printers, televisions, electrical appliances and electronic tools.

Check council or approved recycling information for suitable local drop-off options.
